Battery-aware design for Industial IoT

But ensuring long -- and predictable -- lifetime for battery-dependent IoT nodes requires understanding and optimizing the relationship between node activity and the specific electrochemical dynamics of its energy storage device.

Being founded in Uppsala, Sweden, _Walpurga Systems_ builds on Uppsala University research in battery-aware design for low-power wireless systems, based on application-oriented measurement and system simulation.
